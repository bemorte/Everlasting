# Everlasting rules sheet, prototype v0

**Goal of the run-through:** find gaps and contradictions in the rules, not tune numbers. Numbers come from Balatro and get bent later in the simulator (step 2).

## Run
- 8 Dispensations × 3 Eras: **The Revelation**, **The Kingdom**, **The Faith Crisis** (the Faith Crisis carries a Trial).
- Each Era: beat the target with **4 hands and 3 discards**, hand size 8, play 1–5 cards.
- **Miss a target and the run ends.** Collapse reaching 100 also ends it.
- Targets (Balatro antes): D1 300/450/600 · D2 800/1,200/1,600 · D3 2,000/3,000/4,000 · D4 5,000/7,500/10,000 · D5 11k/16.5k/22k · D6 20k/30k/40k · D7 35k/52.5k/70k · D8 50k/75k/100k.

## Cards and hands
- 52 cards: Command (C), Punish (P), Inspire (I), Reveal (R), ranks A–K. A = 11 Witness, J/Q/K = 10, others face value.
- Score = (hand base Witness + scoring cards' Witness) × hand Glory.
- Hands use **poker names** (Witness × Glory): High Card 5×1 · Pair 10×2 · Two Pair 20×2 · Three of a Kind 30×3 · Straight 30×4 · Flush 35×4 · Full House 40×4 · Four Suits (one of each suit in 5 cards) 45×5 · Four of a Kind 60×7 · Straight Flush 100×8. The religious names move to the Keys that level each hand up (set to be workshopped).
- **Preview before playing:** each scoring card adds 1 to its suit level (bars). Holding shows dial changes per scoring card: Command Agency −2, Progress −1, Collapse −1 · Punish Joy −2, Truth +1, Collapse −2 · Inspire Progress +2, Joy +1, Agency +1 · Reveal Truth +2, Redemption +2, Agency −2.

## Between Eras (narrated by hand for now)
- **Light:** Era reward 3/4/5, +1 per unused hand, +1 interest per 5 held (max 5).
- **Shop:** Godhood Powers (5 slots), Keys (+15 Witness, +1 Glory to a hand type), Tablets (change cards), Packs.
- **Watch (summary turn):** the game master describes the map; the player says where to draw paths and which society-tree trait to buy; believers, deliveries, Prayers and overflow are resolved in one paragraph. Output: a few new cards for the deck and leftover points as Light.
- **After each Faith Crisis's Watch:** the witness pick. Seven people with short bios and exact effects; the chosen one becomes a temporary Power for the next Dispensation and tilts which option fills the next slot.
